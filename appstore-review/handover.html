0 · Standing rules the PO had to repeat today

Rule 2 (made 30 Aug 2026 — the second most important rule in this collaboration). Never use hedging or honesty-signalling phrases. Banned outright:

"I will not guess" · "rather than assume" · "rather than guessing"
"I will not fabricate" · "I could not verify, so…"

State the fact. If something is unknown, name it in one plain clause and move on. He has asked for this repeatedly and seeing it again makes him furious.

English only in every reply, even though he writes in Romanian.

Never end a turn asking "want me to X?" Decide and do it. This is the rule he cares about most.

1 · Where the App Store submission stands

iOS Trunex 1.4.0 (build 19), bundle app.trunex.main, uploaded to App Store Connect on 30 Aug at 14:35 local, on the fourth release run.

Apple sent an "Information Needed" message. They did not reject the app. They asked for seven things: a screen recording made on a physical device starting from app launch and covering registration, login, account deletion, paid flows, user-generated content with reporting and blocking, and every permission prompt; the devices and OS versions tested; what the app does and who it is for; how to reach the main features, with credentials; the external services it depends on; regional differences; and any regulated-industry or third-party-material documentation.

Nothing has been sent to Apple. The PO approves everything before it goes. The drafted reply is Trunex_Reply_to_Apple_v2_31Aug2026.pdf, in his Downloads and committed under appstore-review/ on branch claude/app-store-review-submission-ino4ga of g-1776812341-5630.

Two blanks remain, both held by the PO: the reviewer account's username and password — they exist, he created them deliberately so an Apple reviewer could use the whole app — and the iPhone model and iOS version used for testing and filming.

2 · Two things this session got wrong, corrected

Wrong: "a reviewer hits a wall saying Your application is with us."
Truth: /api/guest/config exists precisely so they do not. The worker's own comment: "Store reviewers open an invitation-only app with no account; this is how they get in." Opening the app gives guest browse mode — map, stories, business, founding. The pending screen appears only after a completed registration. Guest mode locks messaging, SOS, presence, profile, rides and invite.

Wrong: "Trunex is for travellers, expatriates, people far from home."
Truth, from the PO: Trunex is for every person on the planet who needs help in some field, on some subject, at some moment. The person asking need not be the person who needs the help. His example: his daughter Bobo moved to Paris to work in a Michelin restaurant kitchen and needed somewhere trustworthy to rent. He would open Trunex, find vouched members in Paris, and ask them to help her find a place. Neither is a tourist; neither is an expat in the marketing sense. That is a normal Trunex request.

The marketing line, used on the store screenshots for both stores:
"Trunex is a safety net you build before you ever need it." — with ever.

4 · The process decision taken

Do not replace build 19. Apple asked for information, not a code change. On App Store Connect, using a new build means pulling the submission and resubmitting, which loses queue position. Reply against build 19; the Donate fix and the copy fix ride in 1.4.1 after approval.

Committing to the repo does not touch the frozen binary. The only live surface during a review is the backend, which is why no production deploy should happen while a reviewer is exercising the app.
